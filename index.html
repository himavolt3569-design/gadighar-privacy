Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Second Hand Car & Bike application ("Application") provided by the Service Provider.


Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide when using the Application, including your name, email address, phone number, account information, vehicle details, prices, descriptions, and photographs. We may also collect basic device and usage information such as IP address, device type, operating system, and application usage information.


Firebase Services

The Application uses Firebase services provided by Google LLC, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage.

Firebase Authentication is used to manage user accounts and authentication. Cloud Firestore is used to store application data, user information, and vehicle listing information. Firebase Storage is used to store images and files uploaded by users, such as vehicle photographs.


How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide and maintain the Application, manage user accounts and vehicle listings, facilitate vehicle sales and rentals, communicate with users, provide customer support, and improve the Application.


Sharing of Information

We may share information with trusted service providers, including Firebase, when necessary to operate and maintain the Application. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our users, rights, or services.

Information that you choose to publish in a vehicle listing, such as vehicle details, photographs, descriptions, and prices, may be visible to other users.


Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.


Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Second Hand Car & Bike account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at himavolt3569@gmail.com.

To request account deletion, please send an email from the email address associated with your account with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

When we process an account deletion request, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, including account information and associated vehicle listing data, where applicable.

Photos and other files associated with your account may also be deleted from our storage systems.

Some information may be retained when required by law or when reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Any retained information will be kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes.
